# Microsoft MB-310 Study Guide

> **Important correction:** MB-310 is currently **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance Functional Consultant**, not Dynamics 365 Commerce. The guide below follows Microsoft's current official MB-310 objectives, updated August 14, 2026.

## Introduction

Microsoft MB-310 validates functional-consulting skills for implementing and managing financial processes with Dynamics 365 Finance. The exam focuses on translating business requirements into finance solutions covering financial management, accounts receivable, credit and collections, subscription billing, accounts payable, expenses, budgeting, and fixed assets.

Microsoft's current study guide recommends hands-on experience and states that the exam objectives were updated on August 14, 2026.

## Who Should Take It?

MB-310 is suitable for professionals who:

- Work with Dynamics 365 Finance.
- Analyze accounting and financial requirements.
- Configure financial business processes.
- Work with general ledger, accounts payable, or accounts receivable.
- Manage budgeting and fixed assets.
- Support finance implementations and ongoing operations.
- Have familiarity with finance and operations applications.

## Detailed Study Notes

### Financial Dimensions

Learn how financial dimensions provide additional classification for transactions beyond the main account. Practice designing dimensions and configuring account structures and advanced rules.

### General Ledger

Understand ledgers, fiscal calendars, currencies, posting layers, journals, vouchers, allocations, intercompany accounting, and period closing.

### Accounts Receivable

Practice customer configuration, invoices, credit memos, payment processing, posting profiles, credit management, collections, and aging.

### Accounts Payable

Understand vendor setup, invoice matching, validation policies, payment processing, prepayments, recurring invoices, and centralized payments.

### Budgeting

Know the difference between basic budgeting, budget control, and budget planning. Practice comparing budgeted values with actual transactions.

### Fixed Assets

Study the complete asset lifecycle: acquisition, capitalization, depreciation, transfer, reclassification, and disposal.

### Taxes

Review tax codes, tax groups, posting groups, settlement periods, authorities, exemptions, withholding tax, and tax settlement.

## Practical Examples / Labs

Build hands-on scenarios such as:

1. Create a legal entity and configure a chart of accounts.
2. Configure financial dimensions and account structures.
3. Create and post general ledger journals.
4. Configure a bank account and perform reconciliation.
5. Create customers and process invoices and payments.
6. Configure credit and collections workflows.
7. Create vendors and process purchase invoices.
8. Configure expense management and submit an expense report.
9. Create a budget model and compare budget vs. actual values.
10. Create a fixed asset, configure depreciation, and process disposal.
11. Configure sales tax and complete a tax settlement.
12. Practice an end-of-period financial closing process.

## Study Strategy

1. Start with Dynamics 365 Finance fundamentals.
2. Prioritize the 35–40% financial-management domain.
3. Practice configuration instead of memorizing terminology.
4. Learn the end-to-end AR and AP processes.

6. Review taxes, journals, currencies, and financial dimensions.
7. Use Microsoft Learn documentation and training.
8. Complete Microsoft's free practice assessment.
9. Build realistic finance implementation scenarios.
10. Recheck Microsoft's study guide immediately before the exam.

## Common Mistakes

- Memorizing configuration without understanding business processes.
- Confusing main accounts with financial dimensions.
- Ignoring posting profiles.
- Underestimating intercompany scenarios.
- Skipping bank-reconciliation practice.
- Confusing budget control with budget planning.
- Ignoring tax configuration and settlement.
- Failing to understand the complete fixed-asset lifecycle.
- Studying older MB-310 objectives without checking Microsoft's latest version.

## Exam-Day Tips

- Read business scenarios carefully.
- Identify the business requirement before selecting a configuration.
- Watch for differences between customer, vendor, ledger, and asset processes.
- Eliminate options that do not satisfy the stated accounting requirement.
- Pay attention to configuration dependencies.
- Manage time carefully.
- Use Microsoft's exam sandbox before the real exam.
